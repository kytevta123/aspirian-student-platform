**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Final Student Acquisition Blueprint
**Project:** Aspirian Student Platform
**Primary Website:** `aspirian.pk`
**Student Platform:** `app.aspirian.pk`

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the complete student acquisition architecture for Aspirian.

The objective is to build a sustainable system that attracts students through multiple channels and converts them into active Aspirian learners.

# 133. Acquisition Rules

```text
1. Student value comes before conversion.
2. Build organic acquisition as a long-term asset.
3. Use free educational content to establish trust.
4. Use free tools as acquisition engines.
5. Make registration useful, not arbitrary.
6. Get students to their first meaningful learning action quickly.
7. Measure activation, not registration alone.
8. Build retention into the acquisition strategy.
9. Use YouTube and social media to distribute educational value.
10. Use teachers and schools as potential distribution partners.
11. Make useful resources shareable.
12. Use referrals responsibly.
13. Avoid spammy acquisition tactics.
14. Do not fabricate social proof.
15. Do not use misleading SEO or advertising.
16. Respect privacy and student safety.
17. Minimize unnecessary data collection.
18. Track acquisition sources consistently.
19. Measure channel quality, not traffic alone.
20. Optimize the complete funnel.
21. Connect public content with the Student Platform through meaningful CTAs.
22. Keep private student data protected.
23. Use analytics to identify high-value acquisition channels.
24. Build acquisition loops into the product.
25. Treat organic acquisition as a strategic long-term growth asset.
```
