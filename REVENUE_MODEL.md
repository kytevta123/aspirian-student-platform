**Version:** 1.0

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the revenue architecture for the Aspirian ecosystem.

The objective is to build a diversified and sustainable education platform where revenue can come from multiple sources without compromising free educational access.

---

# 2. Revenue Philosophy

Aspirian follows:

```text
Free Education
      +
Premium Learning
      +

      +
Advertising
      =
Sustainable Education Platform
```

# 137. Final Revenue Principles

```text
1. Free education remains central.
2. Advertising monetizes eligible free content.
3. Premium subscriptions monetize advanced value.
4. Schools provide scalable institutional revenue.
5. Premium features must provide genuine learning value.
6. Revenue sources must remain independently measurable.
7. Payment and subscription systems remain separate from revenue analytics.
8. AI costs must be monitored carefully.
9. Pricing must be transparent.
10. Student and parent trust is more important than short-term revenue.
11. Advertising must not compromise learning.
12. Financial data must be access-controlled.
13. Revenue events must be auditable.
14. Refunds and payment fees must be tracked separately.
15. The platform should diversify revenue over time.
16. Revenue decisions should be based on real usage and financial data.
17. Core educational functionality should not depend on paid access.
18. Monetization must remain aligned with student safety and educational quality.
```
